Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a Google API key?

No. The actor works without any Google Places API key.

How is pricing structured?

Pay-per-event. You are charged for each place scraped. Reviews are only charged if you enable the reviews option.

Can I get emails?

Yes. Enable contact enrichment to attempt extraction of emails and social profiles from the business website/text.

Is there a limit on results?

Google typically limits deep pagination to around 120 results per search area. Use multiple targeted searches for larger coverage.
